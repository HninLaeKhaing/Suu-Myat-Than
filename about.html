Honoring Suu Myat Than’s Legacy

In memory of our beloved daughter, Suu Myat Than, whose promising life ended tragically at just 20, we established the Suu Myat Than Education Foundation. Suu embodied extraordinary resilience and dedication, working while studying and saving nearly $20,000 toward her aspirations.

Inspired by her tireless efforts, our family and community rallied, donating an additional $21,000 to seed this foundation. The Suu Myat Than Education Foundation reflects her core values: hard work, compassion, and a profound belief in the transformative power of education for the people of Myanmar.

⚠️

The Crisis in Higher Education

Myanmar's educational system is currently facing a severe and unexpected disruption. These highly unstable conditions, coupled with the sustained collapse of higher education, have forced thousands of students and educators to leave the country.

Without a clear path to fulfilling their academic dreams at home, countless young people have sought refuge and educational opportunities across Southeast Asia, often in countries like Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia. For instance, Ton Duc Thang University in Ho Chi Minh City alone currently hosts over 60 Myanmar students striving to rebuild their futures.

Unfortunately, financial barriers frequently block their access to higher education, turning displacement into desperation.
