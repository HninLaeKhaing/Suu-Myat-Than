Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the Suu Myat Than Education Foundation Scholarship, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Myanmar Background: Applicants must hold Myanmar citizenship or provide documentation confirming prior residency in Myanmar before the current crisis.

  • Accredited Enrollment in Asia: Applicants must be currently enrolled or have received an unconditional offer for full-time study at an accredited higher education institution located in Asia.

  • Demonstrated Financial Need: Applicants must demonstrate significant and verifiable financial need to complete their education due to hardship resulting from conflict or displacement.

  • Academic Commitment: Applicants must show a clear commitment to completing their educational program and provide documentation confirming satisfactory academic standing.

Our Selection Process

1

Prescreening

The selection committee will evaluate applicants’ submissions based on the established criteria. Applicants who achieve a score of 60 or above out of 100 will be invited to a virtual interview via Zoom.

2

Interview

In the second round, committee members will interview shortlisted candidates and assign additional scores of up to 100 points based on their interview performance.

3

Awarding Scholarships

Scholarships will be granted to the applicants with the highest total scores across both rounds.

Selection Criteria

We evaluate applicants based on a holistic assessment of their financial need, academic promise, character, and vision for impact. Below are the ten criteria we use:

Financial Necessity (Priority)

We prioritize applicants facing critical financial challenges that would otherwise prevent them from pursuing or completing their education. Please provide thorough details on the financial barriers impacting your studies.

Academic Excellence

We seek students who demonstrate a proven record of academic achievement. Highlight notable grades, recognition, and any specialized accomplishments in your field of study.

Resilience and Adaptability

We value candidates who have shown exceptional resilience and the ability to thrive despite adversity. Share experiences where you overcame significant obstacles or successfully adjusted to major changes.

Community Impact

We look for individuals who actively contribute to their communities. Please describe your volunteer work, mentorship, or other activities and the tangible positive impact you have made on those around you.

Leadership Potential

We seek applicants who demonstrate leadership skills in formal or informal capacities. Provide specific examples where you led a project, organized an initiative, or motivated others toward a goal.

Passion for Learning

We support students who are genuinely curious and committed to continuous growth. Discuss how your passion drives you to seek knowledge beyond formal coursework and what you hope to achieve through learning.

Clear Educational Goals

We prioritize applicants with a well-defined vision for their academic journey. Clearly articulate your educational and career goals and how receiving this scholarship is a necessary step to realize that vision.

Interpersonal Skills

We value strong communication, collaboration, and empathy. Share experiences demonstrating your ability to work effectively in teams, resolve conflicts, and build positive relationships.

Motivation for the Scholarship

Tell us why this scholarship is essential and what difference it will specifically make in your life and academic progress. We want to support students for whom this award is truly vital.

Potential for Long-Term Impact

We support students whose education will lead to positive change in Myanmar or globally. Share your vision for how your knowledge and skills will enable you to make a lasting difference in your community or field.

Disbursement Schedule

Each recipient will receive a total award of $1,200, divided into two equal installments of $600 to provide consistent support throughout the academic year.

  • 🌸 April: First installment ($600)
  • ☀️ August: Second installment ($600)

We will work with you individually to determine your preferred payment method (e.g., bank transfer, PayPal) and finalize all necessary details before the first payment is made.

Important Notes

  • One-Time Award: This scholarship is a one-time award and is not automatically renewable. Eligible recipients may reapply in future application cycles.
  • Continued Enrollment: Both payments are contingent on your continued enrollment. We may require confirmation of your student status.
  • Tax Responsibility: Scholarship payments may be subject to tax reporting requirements. Consult a tax professional for guidance.
  • Confirmation of Receipt: Please kindly confirm receipt of all scholarship payments once they arrive.
  • Contact Info: Keep your contact information updated with us.
  • Simplified Docs: We gladly accept digital or scanned signatures.
  • Inquiries: For any questions, please do not hesitate to reach out.

Protecting Applicant Privacy

At Suu Myat Than Foundation, we are dedicated to protecting our scholarship applicants' privacy and personal information. To maintain confidentiality, we implemented the following:

Confidentiality Statements

Each selection committee member signs a statement, committing to handling information strictly for selection purposes only.

Data Storage & Retention

Data is securely stored and retained for three years for potential audit or reapplication purposes, after which it is permanently deleted.

Guidance on Sensitive Data

Please avoid providing sensitive info affecting your security (e.g., ID numbers, bank details) unless directly relevant to the selection process.

Transparency

We respect applicants' right to privacy and are transparent about how we use and protect their information.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track the status of my application? ▾

After submitting your application, you will receive an acknowledgment email. All applicants will be notified by email once the selection process concludes in January.

What should I do if I miss the interview or experience technical issues? ▾

If you are unable to attend the interview or face any technical difficulties, please contact us immediately. We will make every effort to reschedule your interview within the available timeframe.

Can I reapply if I don’t receive the scholarship this year? ▾

Yes, applicants who are eligible may reapply in future years.

When will I be notified if I am not selected? ▾

All applicants will be notified of their status by email by the end of January. Unfortunately, we are unable to provide detailed feedback on individual applications.

Will I be given precedence over other applicants if I win an award this year? ▾

No, receiving the scholarship this year does not guarantee any priority in future selection processes. Each year’s applicants are considered based on the same eligibility criteria and selection process. You are welcome to apply again in subsequent years if you remain eligible.
